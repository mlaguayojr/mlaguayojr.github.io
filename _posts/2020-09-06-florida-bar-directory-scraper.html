About

Sometimes coming up with side projects can be difficult. Usually, my source of inspiration for side projects are:

  • learn a new concept, or introduction to a programming language.
  • reduce manual tasks.

  • resolve some problem that started out as annoying (but manageable) but now has my full attention because its now a blocker.


Every so often I'll poke around Reddit (/r/python, or /r/programming) to get see what other poeple are up to. This time, I found a user who was attempting to scrape information from a website. I have experience with this and thus I dove in!
